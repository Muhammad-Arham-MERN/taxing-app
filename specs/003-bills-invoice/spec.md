## Overview

This is the third module of the Income and Expenditure Statement Analysis application, used by a single tax/accounting practitioner on one offline desktop. Modules 1 and 2 gave the practitioner **Statements**: records of money in and out that can be created, stored, reviewed over a date range and corrected. This module adds the other half of the practice's paperwork — **customer Bills (invoices)**.

A bill is composed for one customer, from one or more **line items**, and then turned into a finished, printable invoice. The practitioner enters the customer's details once — or picks a customer billed before and has them filled in — adds as many line items as the work needs, and the application takes care of everything the practitioner should not have to: numbering the items, assigning an invoice number, adding up the line items into a total, and expressing that total in words. The result is a **green-branded PDF invoice** — normally a single page — carrying the practice's own identity — the same logo, name and colour scheme the application already uses — laid out for the client to read and for the practitioner to sign by hand.

Bills are **records like statements**: they live in the same local store file, come back after a restart, can be reviewed over an optional date range, opened and saved to disk, corrected when something is wrong — with the PDF reissued to match — and deleted when they are no longer wanted. Nothing about the module leaves the machine, and nothing about it requires an account, a server or a connection.

The application's navigation gains a **second group of tabs**. The existing "Create" and "View" tabs become **"Create Statement"** and **"View Statements"**, and a parallel **"Create Bill"** and **"View Bills"** pair sits beside them, separated by a small partition so the two kinds of paperwork stay visually distinct while the design remains otherwise unchanged.

This specification describes only what the practitioner can do and observe; how bills are composed into a PDF, and how they are stored and numbered, belong to the plan rather than here.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-09-18

- Q: When the "+" action adds another item, does it add only a line item or duplicate the whole block including the customer details? → A: **Only a line item.** The customer details are entered once per bill and shared by all of its line items, matching the invoice layout, which shows a single customer-details block above the line-item table.
- Q: The client asked for a single-page invoice and also for as many line items as the bill needs. What should happen when the line items exceed what fits on one page? → A: **Continue onto further pages, without losing content.** A single page remains the design target because the practitioner sells services, not products, and normally raises bills with one to a few items; the extra pages exist only for the "just in case" case and never truncate a bill.
- Q: The client marked Date, Details and Customer Name with "*" but not Amount, yet a bill with no amount totals zero. Which entries gate the Create Bill action? → A: **Date, each line item's Details, each line item's Amount, and the Customer Name, with at least one line item required.** A bill can never be created with a missing or zero line-item amount.
- Q: The client asked for amounts "in PKR and comma separated throughout application" but also asked to keep the existing Rs format where it is present. How should amounts be presented? → A: **Bills present amounts in PKR with comma thousands separators; the existing Statements presentation (rupee symbol, Module 1 FR-033) is left exactly as it is.** The global change is therefore limited to the new Bills feature, and Module 1 FR-033 is no longer superseded.
- Q: Is a bill's generated invoice stored, or recreated from the bill's data? → A: **Regenerated on demand.** Only the bill's own values and its invoice number are kept in the store; the invoice PDF is rendered afresh whenever it is viewed or downloaded, so it can never go stale against the bill.
- Q: How is a bill's invoice PDF made available to the practitioner? → A: **Opened in the system's default PDF application**, matching how statement attachments already open, with no PDF viewer bundled into the application.
- Q: What does the View Bills list show, and how can bills be narrowed down? → A: **Columns Customer Name, Date, Details and Amount**, with the optional date range joined by a **Customer filter** — a drop-down of every customer used before that can also be typed into — so the list shows one customer's bills within whatever date range is chosen.
- Q: What form should the automatically generated invoice number take? → A: **Sequential with a prefix, for example `INV-0001`**, incrementing and never reused, so a number freed by a deleted bill is never handed to another.
- Q: Are customer details retyped on every bill, or remembered? → A: **Remembered.** Entering a customer name saves that customer in the store, and the practitioner can pick a customer billed before to fill their details in on a new bill.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Create a Bill and Receive Its Invoice PDF (Priority: P1)

The practitioner has finished a piece of work for a client and needs to bill them. They open **Create Bill**, enter the date (already filled with today), the customer's name and details, and the work that was done — a description and an amount for each line. One piece of work is not enough for this client, so they press **"+"** and a second line item appears, and then a third. As they type, the item numbers run 1, 2, 3, the total is added up for them, and the total is spelled out in words. Only one line item's description is still blank, so the **Create Bill** button stays greyed out; once it is filled the button becomes available. They press it, and a green invoice PDF appears, ready to be printed or sent, carrying the practice's header, the customer's details and the invoice number and date, the table of line items, the signature line, and the footer of bank and wallet details.

**Why this priority**: This is the entire reason the module exists. Without the ability to compose a bill and obtain its invoice, nothing else here has any value; on its own it turns the application into a tool the practitioner can actually bill clients with, so it is a viable product by itself.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by opening Create Bill, entering one valid bill with two or more line items, and confirming the Create Bill action was unavailable until the required entries were complete and that a correct green invoice PDF is produced with the header, customer block, line-item table, signature line and footer.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the application is open with a usable store, **When** the practitioner looks at the navigation, **Then** they see a Statements group offering "Create Statement" and "View Statements" and a Bills group, separated by a small partition, offering "Create Bill" and "View Bills".
2. **Given** the Create Bill tab is open and untouched, **When** the practitioner looks at the form, **Then** the date shows today, the account holder name shows "Mumtaz Qureshi", the customer-detail fields are present, and the Create Bill action is unavailable.
3. **Given** the Create Bill tab is open, **When** the practitioner looks at the form, **Then** every entry they are expected to provide is visible, with the required ones shown above the optional ones, and the automatically generated values (item number, total, amount in words, invoice number) are not shown for them to fill in.
4. **Given** a line item is present, **When** the practitioner activates "+", **Then** another line item is added and the item numbers are renumbered in order (1, 2, 3, …).
5. **Given** the practitioner has entered two or more line items with amounts, **When** they look at the total, **Then** it equals the sum of the line items and is presented in PKR with comma separators.
6. **Given** a total has been computed, **When** the practitioner looks at the amount in words, **Then** it is the total expressed in words.
7. **Given** a required entry (the date, a line item's details, a line item's amount, or the customer name) is missing or invalid, or the bill has no line item, **When** the practitioner looks at the form, **Then** the Create Bill action is unavailable and nothing can be submitted.
8. **Given** every required entry is complete and valid, **When** the practitioner activates Create Bill, **Then** the bill is stored and its green invoice opens in the system's default PDF application, normally on a single page.
9. **Given** an invoice PDF has been produced, **When** the practitioner reads it, **Then** it shows the practice's header, a customer-details block with the customer's details in one column and the invoice number and date in an adjacent column, and a table with the columns Item Number, Details, Amount(s), Total and Amount in Words.
10. **Given** an invoice PDF has been produced, **When** the practitioner reads below the table, **Then** they see, centred and bold, "For & On Behalf of M&M Tax Law Solutions" followed by a blank line for a physical signature.
11. **Given** an invoice PDF has been produced, **When** the practitioner reads the footer, **Then** it shows "Meezan: 03090100464603", "JS Bank 0002717402 (M&M Tax Law Solutions)", the JazzCash number they entered and the Easypaisa number they entered.
12. **Given** a bill is created, **When** the application is closed and reopened, **Then** that bill is still there with the same values and produces the same invoice.

### User Story 2 - Review and Filter Bills (Priority: P2)

At the end of a month the practitioner opens **View Bills** to see what they have invoiced. They leave the filters empty because they want the whole picture, and every bill they have raised is listed newest first, showing each one's customer, date, details and amount. For a specific client enquiry they pick that customer from the filter — or start typing the name — and set a From and a To date, and the list narrows to exactly that customer's bills inside that period, newest first.

**Why this priority**: Reviewing what has been billed is what makes a bill archive useful and is needed to find a bill to open, correct or delete. It sits below creation only because there is nothing to review until bills can be created, and it depends on that capability.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by creating bills for several customers on different dates, opening View Bills with no filters and confirming all bills appear newest first with customer, date, details and amount, then combining a chosen customer with a date range and confirming exactly that customer's bills inside it (inclusive of both ends) are listed, newest first.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the View Bills tab is open and no date range or customer has been chosen, **When** the list is shown, **Then** every stored bill is listed, newest first.
2. **Given** bills are listed, **When** the practitioner reads a row, **Then** it shows the bill's Customer Name, Date, Details and Amount.
3. **Given** stored bills span many dates, **When** the practitioner selects a From and a To date, **Then** exactly the bills dated within that inclusive range are listed, newest first.
4. **Given** the practitioner chooses a customer from the Customer filter, **When** the list is shown, **Then** only that customer's bills are listed, newest first, across all dates.
5. **Given** the practitioner starts typing part of a name into the Customer filter, **When** they look at the offered customers, **Then** the previously used customers matching what they typed are offered for selection.
6. **Given** a customer is chosen and a date range is set, **When** the list is shown, **Then** only that customer's bills dated within the range are listed, newest first.
7. **Given** a customer or range is chosen and no bills match, **When** the results are shown, **Then** the list communicates that there are no bills for that choice.
8. **Given** the practitioner clears the chosen customer and range, **When** the results are shown, **Then** all bills are listed again, newest first.
9. **Given** the From date is later than the To date, **When** the practitioner looks at the results, **Then** the range is reported as invalid rather than shown as an empty result.
10. **Given** the practitioner has billed a customer before, **When** they open the Customer filter, **Then** that customer is offered in the drop-down even if every bill raised for them has been deleted.
11. **Given** bills are listed, **When** the practitioner selects one, **Then** that bill's values and its invoice can be opened.

### User Story 3 - Open, Correct and Re-issue a Bill (Priority: P3)

The practitioner opens a bill from View Bills and realises the customer's address is wrong, or an amount was typed incorrectly, or a line item should be added. They open the bill, change the fields that need changing — including adding or removing line items — and save. The application updates the same bill in place and regenerates the invoice PDF so it matches; nothing is duplicated and the corrected invoice is still there after a restart. When they need a copy outside the application, they download the PDF to a folder of their choosing.

**Why this priority**: Real billing always needs corrections, and an invoice that cannot be fixed means re-issuing by hand. It is below creation and review because it only becomes meaningful once bills exist and can be found.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by creating a bill, opening it from View Bills, changing its customer details and line items, saving, and confirming the same bill (not a new one) holds the new values and its regenerated PDF reflects them across a restart, and that the PDF can be downloaded.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a bill is listed in View Bills, **When** the practitioner opens it, **Then** every stored value is prefilled and editable.
2. **Given** the practitioner changes a bill's fields and saves, **Then** the existing bill is updated in place — no additional bill is created — and the invoice PDF is regenerated to match.
3. **Given** the practitioner adds or removes a line item while editing and saves, **Then** the total and the amount in words are recalculated and the regenerated PDF reflects the new line items.
4. **Given** the practitioner abandons an edit, **Then** the stored bill and its invoice PDF remain exactly as they were.
5. **Given** a corrected bill, **When** the application is closed and reopened, **Then** the corrected values and the regenerated PDF are what the practitioner sees.
6. **Given** the practitioner wants the invoice outside the application, **When** they choose to download it, **Then** the system's save prompt appears and the saved file is the invoice PDF.

### User Story 4 - Delete a Bill (Priority: P4)

A bill was raised by mistake, or a duplicate was created. The practitioner opens **View Bills**, selects that bill, and deletes it. The bill and its invoice PDF are removed from the store and from the list.

**Why this priority**: Mistaken bills must be removable so the archive reflects reality. It is the lowest priority because it is a rare, corrective action that only becomes possible once bills can be created and found.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by creating a bill, deleting it from View Bills, and confirming it no longer appears in any list and does not return after a restart.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a bill is listed in View Bills, **When** the practitioner deletes it, **Then** the bill and its invoice PDF are removed from the store and from the list.
2. **Given** a bill has been deleted, **When** the application is closed and reopened, **Then** the bill does not return.
3. **Given** the practitioner is deleting a bill, **Then** they are asked to confirm before the bill is removed.
4. **Given** the practitioner deletes a bill, **Then** no stored Statement is affected.

### Edge Cases

- **Renaming the existing tabs**: "Create" and "View" become "Create Statement" and "View Statements", and the Statements screens keep behaving exactly as before; only the labels and the navigation partition change.
- **A bill with a single line item**: a bill is valid with one line item; "+" is only needed for a second and beyond.
- **Removing down to one line item**: a bill must never be left with zero line items; the last remaining item cannot be removed, only cleared.
- **Item numbering after a removal**: removing a middle item renumbers the remaining items 1, 2, 3 … with no gaps.
- **Zero or non-numeric amount**: a line item's amount must be a valid number; the Create Bill action stays unavailable while any amount is invalid.
- **Very large amount**: amounts larger than typical must be added up, spelled out and displayed without truncation or loss of precision.
- **Many line items**: a bill with many line items must remain usable to enter and must produce a correct total and amount in words.
- **Content longer than one page**: the invoice is laid out to fit one page for a typical bill; when the line items exceed that, the invoice continues onto further pages without dropping or truncating any content (FR-028).
- **Optional customer details left empty**: customer details that were not provided must render clearly on the invoice without leaving broken or misleading gaps.
- **JazzCash or Easypaisa left empty**: the footer must still render correctly when either wallet number was not provided.
- **Account holder name cleared**: clearing the account holder name leaves it at its default "Mumtaz Qureshi" rather than blank.
- **Reversed range in View Bills**: a From date later than the To date is reported as invalid rather than showing an empty result.
- **No bills at all**: View Bills with no stored bills shows a clear empty state and does not look broken.
- **Invoice numbers over time**: invoice numbers must stay unique as bills are added and must never be reused, and a bill that is corrected keeps the invoice number it was issued with rather than being renumbered. A number belonging to a deleted bill is never handed to another bill.
- **Customer name reused**: entering a name that already exists refers to that same customer rather than creating a second customer under the same name; their stored details are offered and kept up to date.
- **Customer with no remaining bills**: a customer whose bills have all been deleted is still offered by the Customer filter; choosing them simply produces an empty list.
- **Filter matches nothing**: choosing a customer and a range that together match no bill shows the empty state rather than an error.
- **Typing a partial name**: a partially typed name offers the stored customers that match, so the practitioner need not recall the exact spelling.
- **Customer details corrected on a bill**: changing a customer's details while saving a bill updates the details held for that customer, so later bills start from the corrected values.
- **Interrupted save**: if the application or machine stops mid-save, no half-written bill may appear — a bill is either stored completely or not at all.
- **Repeated submission**: activating Create Bill several times quickly must still store exactly one bill.
- **Deleting a bill seen earlier**: a bill already deleted must not reappear from a stale list, and deleting the same bill twice must not remove another bill.
- **Cancelling a download**: dismissing the save prompt leaves the stored bill and its PDF unchanged.
- **Regenerating an unchanged bill**: re-saving a bill whose fields did not change must not create duplicates or change its invoice number.
- **Amounts in the two areas**: Bills use the PKR, comma-separated presentation; Statements keep the rupee-symbol presentation they already have, so the two sit side by side rather than one replacing the other.
- **Store not usable**: as elsewhere in the application, no bill can be created, reviewed, edited or deleted until a usable store exists.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

**Navigation**

- **FR-001**: The navigation MUST present two labelled groups — **Statements** and **Bills** — inside the same container, separated by a **gap** (not a dividing rule), each keeping the existing design (client change, 2026-09-19).
- **FR-002**: The Statements group MUST offer a "Create" tab and a "View" tab, keeping the existing statements behaviour unchanged.
- **FR-003**: The Bills group MUST offer a "Create" tab and a "View" tab.
- **FR-004**: Selecting any tab MUST show the corresponding screen without disturbing the others.

**Bill capture**

- **FR-005**: The Create Bill tab MUST present a form following the same component style used to create statements, headed for creating a bill.
- **FR-006**: The form MUST include a Date entry that defaults to the current date and can be changed by the practitioner.
- **FR-007**: The form MUST include a free-text Details entry and a numeric Amount entry for each line item.
- **FR-008**: The form MUST include customer entries for Customer Name, Customer Address, Contact Person, Contact Number, Email, NTN Number and Password.
- **FR-009**: The form MUST include a JazzCash number entry and an Easypaisa number entry, each with a "+" that adds **as many further numbers as the practitioner wants** (client change, 2026-09-19). All are optional, but when given each MUST be entered in +92 format, and a number that is not valid MUST be explained to the practitioner rather than only leaving the Create action unavailable. A new bill MUST start with the practice's own numbers already in place — `+923225739614` for JazzCash and `+923125739614` for Easypaisa — which the practitioner can clear and replace with another (client request, 2026-09-19). These, together with the Account Holder Name, MUST be presented in their own "Payment Details" section, separate from the customer's own details (FR-008).
- **FR-010**: The form MUST include an Account Holder Name entry defaulting to "Mumtaz Qureshi".
- **FR-011**: The entries the practitioner must fill in MUST be shown with the required ones above the optional ones.
- **FR-012**: Automatically generated values — the item numbers, the total, the amount in words and the invoice number — MUST NOT be presented as entries for the practitioner to fill in.
- **FR-013**: A "+" action MUST add another line item to the bill, and the practitioner MUST be able to add as many line items as the bill needs. The customer details MUST be entered once per bill and shared by all of its line items; adding a line item MUST NOT duplicate the customer details.
- **FR-014**: Each line item MUST be numbered automatically and in order, one after another, with no gaps and no duplicates.
- **FR-015**: A line item MUST be removable, and removing one MUST renumber the remaining items in order.
- **FR-016**: The form MUST NOT allow a bill to be left with no line items.
- **FR-017**: The bill's Total MUST be calculated automatically as the sum of its line items' amounts, and MUST be recalculated whenever the line items or their amounts change.
- **FR-018**: The bill's total MUST be presented in words, generated automatically and kept consistent with the total. The wording MUST use English numbering (hundred, thousand, million, billion), not lakh or crore (client change, 2026-09-19).
- **FR-019**: Each bill MUST be assigned an automatically generated invoice number in a sequential, prefixed form (for example `INV-0001`) that is unique among stored bills and is not entered by the practitioner. An invoice number MUST NOT be reused: a number that belonged to a deleted bill MUST NOT be issued to another bill.
- **FR-020**: The Create Bill action MUST remain unavailable until every required entry is complete and valid.
- **FR-021**: The entries required before a bill may be created are the Date, each line item's Details, each line item's Amount, and the Customer Name, and the bill MUST have at least one line item. A bill MUST NOT be creatable with a missing or zero line-item amount.
- **FR-022**: Activating Create Bill MUST store the bill durably before it is reported as created, and MUST NOT leave a partially stored bill if it is interrupted.
- **FR-023**: Repeated rapid activation of Create Bill MUST still result in exactly one stored bill.
- **FR-024**: After a bill is created, the form MUST reset to its initial state with the date defaulted to today and the account holder name at its default.
- **FR-025**: When a bill cannot be stored, the practitioner MUST be told it was not created, and the form MUST NOT behave as though the creation succeeded.

**Amount presentation**

- **FR-026**: Bills MUST present every amount in PKR with comma thousands separators. The existing Statement amount presentation (Module 1 FR-033 — the rupee symbol with thousands separators and two decimals) MUST be left unchanged, so Module 1 FR-033 remains in force and the PKR presentation is confined to the new Bills feature.

**Invoice PDF**

- **FR-027**: A bill's invoice MUST be produced as a PDF on demand — when the bill is created, viewed, downloaded, or after an edit is saved — rendered from the bill's stored values. The invoice PDF's bytes MUST NOT be kept in the store; only the bill's values and its invoice number are stored, so the invoice always matches the bill and can never go stale.
- **FR-028**: The invoice PDF MUST use the application's green colour scheme, rounded elements, the same logo and company name, and a legible typeface, and MUST be laid out to fit a single page for a typical bill. When a bill's line items exceed what fits on one page, the invoice MUST continue onto as many further pages as needed so that no content is lost or truncated.
- **FR-029**: The invoice header MUST show the M&M company name, its details and its contacts.
- **FR-030**: Below the header, the invoice MUST show a customer-details block with Customer Name, Address, Contact Person, Contact Number, Email and NTN Number in one column, and the Invoice Number and Date in an adjacent column. An optional detail that was not provided MUST read "N/A" on the invoice.
- **FR-031**: Below the customer-details block, the invoice MUST show a table with the columns Item Number, Details, Amount(s) and Total, listing the bill's line items. The bill's **amount in words** MUST be shown below the table, on the left (client change, 2026-09-19).
- **FR-032**: Below the table, the invoice MUST show "For & On Behalf of M&M Tax Law Solutions" with, beneath a large gap, a subtle **unlabelled** line on the left for the practitioner's signature and a subtle line on the right labelled "Customer Acknowledgement" (client change, 2026-09-19).
- **FR-033**: The invoice footer MUST lay the payment details out as four **structural columns** that align but draw no table borders — "Meezan Bank" with "03090100464603", "JS Bank" with "0002717402 (M&M Tax Law Solutions)", "Easypaisa" and "JazzCash" — with each wallet's numbers listed beneath its heading in bold, readable text. The bill's **account holder name** MUST be written beside the Easypaisa and JazzCash columns, so the practitioner's wallet details are complete on the invoice (client change, 2026-09-19).

### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **Bill**: A customer invoice raised by the practitioner. Key attributes: an automatically assigned, sequential, never-reused invoice number (for example `INV-0001`), a date, one or more line items, a customer (by name) and their details (address, contact person, contact number, email, NTN number), the payment details shown in the footer (JazzCash number, Easypaisa number, account holder name), and the total of its line items with that total in words. It is stored in the same store as statements, can be corrected in place, and is deleted only when the practitioner asks.
- **Line Item**: One piece of work on a bill. Key attributes: its position/number on the bill, its details, and its amount. A bill has at least one; they are numbered in order and renumbered when one is removed.
- **Customer**: A person or business the practitioner has billed, identified by name. Key attributes: name, address, contact person, contact number, email, NTN number and password. A customer is remembered in the store the first time their name is entered on a bill, can be chosen to fill in the detail fields of a new bill, is offered by the View Bills Customer filter even when none of their bills remain, and has their own details viewed and edited from the popup opened by clicking their name in the list.
- **Invoice**: The rendered, green PDF produced for a bill — normally a single page — with header, customer-details block, line-item table, signature line and footer. It is rendered on demand from the bill's stored values rather than stored itself, so it always matches the bill; it opens in the system's default PDF application and can be saved to disk.
- **Store**: The single self-contained local file already used for statements, which now holds bills and the customers used to raise them as well. It remains the practitioner's own, offline, and reachable only from their machine.

## Assumptions

- **Scope boundary**: This module covers composing customer bills, automatically numbering and totalling their line items, generating, opening and downloading a green invoice PDF, reviewing and filtering bills by customer and optional date range, remembering customers so their details can be reused, correcting bills in place, deleting bills, and storing bills and customers alongside statements in the existing local store. Out of scope: any change to how statements work beyond their tab labels, reporting or exports beyond saving a single invoice PDF, a wider customer-management area beyond remembering names and their details for reuse, the analytics/processing modules, multi-user access, and any other change to the application's design or layout. Statement deletion remains out of scope.
- **Amount presentation**: The client asked for amounts in PKR with comma separators and also asked to keep the existing Rs (rupee-symbol) format where it is present. Both are honoured: Bills present amounts in PKR with comma thousands separators, and the existing Statement presentation (Module 1 FR-033) is left exactly as it is, so Module 1 FR-033 is not superseded. Two decimal places are retained for consistency unless the client directs otherwise. Confirmed by the client in the 2026-09-18 clarification.
- **Required entries**: The Date, each line item's Details, each line item's Amount and the Customer Name are required, and a bill must have at least one line item; the Create Bill action stays unavailable until all of these are satisfied. Confirmed by the client in the 2026-09-18 clarification.
- **The repeated "+" component**: The "+" action adds a *line item* (Details + Amount) beneath one set of customer details shared by the whole bill, per the client's 2026-09-18 clarification. The customer details are never duplicated; the invoice shows a single customer-details block.
- **Single-page invoice**: A single page is the design target, since the practitioner sells services rather than products and normally raises bills with one to a few items. The client confirmed in the 2026-09-18 clarification that when a bill's line items exceed one page the invoice continues onto further pages, so content is never truncated or lost.
- **"Rounded figures"**: Read as a visual instruction (rounded shapes in the invoice design), not as rounding the amounts.
- **Total and amount in words**: The total is the sum of the line items' amounts. The amount in words expresses that bill total, and the invoice table carries an Amount in Words column as the client specified.
- **Invoice numbering**: Invoice numbers are generated by the application so the practitioner never types one. They are sequential with a prefix (for example `INV-0001`), unique across stored bills, retained when a bill is corrected, and never reused even after a bill is deleted. Confirmed by the client in the 2026-09-18 clarification.
- **Invoice rendering and viewing**: Only the bill's values and its invoice number are kept; the invoice PDF is rendered on demand, so a copy can never be stale against the bill it belongs to. It opens in the system's default PDF application — the same way statement attachments already open — and can be saved to disk, with no PDF viewer bundled into the application. Confirmed by the client in the 2026-09-18 clarification.
- **Customer directory**: The customer names entered on bills are remembered in the same store, and a customer's details can be reused to fill in a new bill. Customers are identified by name, so an existing name refers to the existing customer rather than creating a duplicate; changing a customer's details on a bill updates the details held for them, and a customer stays offered by the filter even after all of their bills are deleted. This is the feature the client confirmed as in scope in the 2026-09-18 clarification.
- **Payment details on the invoice**: The JazzCash number, Easypaisa number and account holder name are entered once per bill and shown in the invoice footer; the account holder name defaults to "Mumtaz Qureshi" and returns to that default after a bill is created.
- **Invalidation of a bill's PDF**: The invoice is a rendering of the bill's current values, so correcting a bill reissues its invoice rather than preserving the superseded one. The practice's fixed details (company name, contacts, the two bank lines) are the same constants the application already uses for its header.
- **Bills are independent of statements**: A bill does not create or modify a Statement, and no statement produces a bill; the two kinds of record simply share the one store file.
- **Storage and offline**: Bills use the same single self-contained local store file as statements and are subject to the same fully-offline, single-user, single-machine rules already established (including the storage gate that must be satisfied before any work can be done).
- **Verification approach**: Consistent with Module 2, the backend surface is small and the client has directed that acceptance is verified by exercising the application end to end; the existing frontend tests must keep passing.
- **Existing data**: There are no existing bills to migrate; the store simply begins holding bills from the first one created after this module ships, alongside the statements it already holds.
